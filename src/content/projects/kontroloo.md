I co-founded Kontroloo in 2017, in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. We built NFC-based access control and cashless payments for festivals and live events.

The bet was that one wristband could do more than open a gate: cut organisers' costs, remove fraud from the event's cash flow and give attendees a smoother experience.
