I co-founded AIOS in 2025 and worked on it until August 2026. Our clients were mostly Spanish SMEs that wanted a competitive edge from AI; we worked with more than 10 of them.

The idea was to productise it: an organisational OS that reshapes how a company is organised to put AI at the centre. In practice it turned into a conventional agency, and that wasn't what we had set out to build.
